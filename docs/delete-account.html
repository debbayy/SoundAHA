Delete your SoundAHA account

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open SoundAHA and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Make sure you are logged in, then tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted right away.

Without the app

Email CONTACT_EMAIL from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my SoundAHA account". We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and login).
  • Your synced favorites.
  • Any party files you uploaded (these are also deleted automatically within about 6 hours).

What is not affected

Songs, playlists and settings stored on your phone are never sent to us, so they stay on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Server logs kept by our hosting provider for security are removed on its normal schedule.
