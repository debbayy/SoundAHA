Privacy Policy

SoundAHA · Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what data the SoundAHA app ("the app", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. The developer is [Developer name], contact: CONTACT_EMAIL.

Data that stays on your phone

Songs you import, playlists, play history, favorites, settings and the song you were last playing are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

Data we process

  • Account (optional). If you create an account: your email address and a password (stored hashed by our authentication provider). Used to log you in and sync favorites.
  • Synced favorites. When logged in, the IDs of sounds from our catalog that you favorite, so they follow your account.
  • Party (optional). When you join a party: the display name you choose and a random session ID, shared with the other members of that party while it runs. If you are logged in and play a song from your phone during a party, that audio file is uploaded to private storage so the other members can play it. It is reachable only through links that expire after 6 hours and is deleted when you leave the party, or at the latest about 6 hours after upload.
  • Online sound search. Search words you type are sent to our server, which asks Freesound.org and caches the results. Searches are not linked to your account.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider processes IP addresses and request logs as needed to deliver the service and protect it from abuse.

We do not show ads, we do not use analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell or share personal data for advertising.

Device permissions

  • Files / audio: only the files or folder you pick, to import songs.
  • Notifications / background playback: to show playback controls and keep playing with the screen off.
  • Internet: for the catalog, account, search and party features.

Service providers

  • Supabase (database, authentication, storage, realtime): stores the account data, synced favorites and temporary party files described above.
  • Freesound.org: provides online search results and sound previews, played directly from Freesound.

Retention and deletion

Account data and synced favorites are kept until you delete your account. Party files are deleted within about 6 hours. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Delete account) or by following the steps on the account deletion page.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Server data is protected by access rules so each user can only reach their own data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and update the date above.
